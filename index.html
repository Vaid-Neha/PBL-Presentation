AI Digital Forensic Agent for Deepfake Detection

Develop an AI-based Digital Forensic Agent capable of detecting deepfake manipulations in images and videos using multimodal analysis and explainable AI techniques.

Submitted By: Neha Vaid (2427030732)

Supervised By: Dr. Gireesh Kumar

Problem Statement

Deepfakes are AI-generated synthetic media created using GANs and autoencoders. These manipulations threaten digital authenticity, enabling misinformation, cybercrime, and identity theft. Traditional forensic tools fail against modern high-quality deepfakes, requiring AI-powered detection systems.

Research Gap

• No model computes audio-visual consistency score
• Poor performance on compressed real-world videos
• Lack of explainable AI for forensic validation
• Weak robustness across datasets and deepfake types
• No unified multimodal detection framework

Proposed Model: AVXF-Net

A multimodal AI model combining CNN (visual), Bi-LSTM (temporal), and audio CNN with cross-modal attention to compute an audio-visual consistency score. Includes explainable AI (Grad-CAM + SHAP) for forensic interpretation.
