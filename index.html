Existing Models & Performance

Model Type Performance Key Strength
CNN (XceptionNet) Vision CNN ~97% AUC (FF++), ~65% (DFDC) High spatial accuracy
LSTM Models Temporal DL ~91% (Celeb-DF) Captures motion inconsistencies
Transformers ViT / TimeSformer ~11% drop cross-dataset Better generalization
Audio CNN Audio DL Moderate Detects voice anomalies
SyncNet Cross-modal Effective for lip-sync Audio-video alignment

Limitations of Existing Models

  • • Poor cross-dataset generalization (up to 15% accuracy drop)
  • • Most models are unimodal (only video or only audio)
  • • Fail under compression (social media quality)
  • • High computational cost (Transformers not real-time)
  • • No explainability (black-box predictions)

Research Gaps Identified

  • • No model computes audio-visual consistency score
  • • No real-time detection under compressed videos
  • • Lack of explainable AI for forensic evidence
  • • Weak robustness to GAN + diffusion deepfakes
  • • No unified multimodal detection framework

Proposed Model: AVXF-Net

A novel multimodal deepfake detection model combining visual, temporal, and audio analysis using cross-modal attention and explainable AI.

  • • CNN (EfficientNet) for visual features
  • • Bi-LSTM for temporal modeling
  • • Audio CNN (MFCC + Spectrogram)
  • • Cross-modal attention for alignment scoring
  • • Grad-CAM + SHAP for explainability
