Problem Statement

The Crisis

Deepfakes — AI-synthesized media using GANs, autoencoders, and diffusion models — threaten digital authenticity, judicial evidence, national security, and public trust. Traditional forensic tools are completely outpaced by modern synthesis techniques.

› $25.6M lost in a single deepfake video-call fraud (Arup, 2024)
› AI robocall impersonated President Biden during NH primary (2024)
› 96% of deepfakes online are non-consensual synthetic media
› New deepfake posted every 5 minutes globally

Why Existing Tools Fail

› CNN models drop 30%+ accuracy under social-media compression

› Transformer models are 3–5× too slow for real-time use

› SyncNet evaded by perfectly synced Wav2Lip deepfakes

› Zero existing models produce court-admissible explanations

› GAN-trained detectors completely blind to diffusion-model fakes

Impact on Digital Forensics

› Fabricated video evidence undermines judicial systems

› Biometric authentication (face + voice) rendered unreliable

› Misinformation at unprecedented scale and realism

› Legal systems lack standards for AI-generated evidence

03

Research Gaps & Innovation

1

No Audio-Visual Consistency Scoring

SyncNet only checks phoneme-lip timing. No model quantifies semantic and emotional audio-visual alignment simultaneously — emotionally inconsistent deepfakes evade all current detectors.

→ AVXF-Net introduces cross-modal attention consistency score
2

No Real-Time Detection Under Compression

CNNs degrade 30%+ on H.264 compressed video. Transformers are too slow (>500ms). No model jointly optimizes accuracy + latency + compression robustness for social-media deployment.

→ AVXF-Net targets <150ms inference with H.264 augmented training
3

Zero Forensic Explainability (XAI)

All reviewed models output a binary label only. No model produces heatmaps, frame-level annotations, or audio attributions usable as court-admissible forensic evidence.

→ AVXF-Net adds Grad-CAM + SHAP dual-stream XAI module
4

GAN-Trained Models Blind to Diffusion Deepfakes

Models trained on GAN artifacts fail completely on Stable Diffusion, Sora, and DALL-E generated content — fundamentally different artifact spaces that existing datasets don't cover.

→ Multimodal consistency scoring is generator-agnostic

The Innovation — What Makes AVXF-Net Different

First model to combine:

Visual CNN + Temporal LSTM + Audio CNN + Cross-modal Attention + XAI — all in one lightweight pipeline

Novel metric introduced:

Audio-Visual Consistency Score (AVCS) — a quantitative forensic signal not present in any existing published work

Suitable publication venues:

IEEE TIFS · ACM MM · CVPR Forensics Workshop · IJCAI 2026

3-Phase Training Strategy

PHASE 1 — 5 epochs each
Pretrain Encoders

Visual: Fine-tune EfficientNet-B4 on FF++ frame-level

Audio: Train on VoxCeleb2 vs. DeepfakeTIMIT

LR: 1e-4 with cosine annealing

PHASE 2 — 10 epochs
Train Fusion Head

Frozen encoders; train Cross-Attn + Transformer + Classifier

Loss: BCE + MSE consistency (self-supervised)

Batch: 16–32 clips / GPU

PHASE 3 — 15 epochs
End-to-End Fine-tuning

All modules unfrozen; LR reduced to 1e-5

Loss: 0.7 × BCE + 0.3 × Consistency

Optimizer: AdamW + OneCycleLR
