AI Digital Forensic Agent for Deepfake Detection

Neha Vaid | Dr. Gireesh Kumar

Problem Statement

Deepfakes are highly realistic AI-generated media that threaten authenticity and security. Traditional detection methods fail against modern techniques.

Literature Review

  • CNN (XceptionNet) – High accuracy but poor generalization
  • LSTM – Temporal analysis but slow
  • Transformer – Good generalization but heavy
  • SyncNet – Lip-sync detection only

Research Gap

  • No audio-visual consistency scoring
  • No explainability
  • Poor performance on compressed data

Proposed Model

AVXF-Net combines CNN, LSTM, and audio models with cross-modal attention and explainability.

Implementation Plan

  • Data collection (FF++, DFDC)
  • Frame & audio extraction
  • Feature extraction (CNN, MFCC)
  • Model training
  • Explainability integration
  • Deployment using Streamlit

Expected Results

High accuracy (~95%) with improved robustness and explainability.
